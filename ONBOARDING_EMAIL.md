## 🎯 What is UpiSense?

UpiSense automatically tracks your **UPI payments** by analyzing the notifications you forward via WhatsApp. No app download. No manual entry. Just forward → we parse & categorize.

## 🚀 Getting Started (3 Steps)

### Step 1: Create Your Account
Reply to this email with your phone number in this format:
```
Phone: +919876543210
```

We'll send you an OTP to verify.

### Step 2: Activate Your Account  
Once you receive the OTP, reply with:
```
OTP: [6-digit code]
```

You'll get a confirmation and access token.

### Step 3: Start Forwarding Transactions
When you receive a UPI payment notification:
1. Forward it to our WhatsApp bot: **+1-201-555-0123**
2. We'll parse it instantly
3. It gets saved to your profile

**Example transactions to forward**:
- ✓ "Rs. 500 paid to Zomato via GPay on 21-Feb..."
- ✓ "Payment of Rs.1,250 to Swiggy is successful..."
- ✓ "A/C X5678 debited by Rs 300..."

## ❓ Quick FAQ

**Q: Will this slow down my WhatsApp?**  
A: No! We process notifications in the background. Takes 5-10 seconds per message.

**Q: Is my data safe?**  
A: Yes. All data is encrypted. We store only metadata (amount, merchant, timestamp). Never share OTPs or payment methods.

**Q: What happens if a transaction doesn't parse?**  
A: We collect these samples to improve. Just let us know the original message text.

**Q: When do I get the dashboard?**  
A: Week 3 (by March 7). You'll see all your transactions with charts and insights.
